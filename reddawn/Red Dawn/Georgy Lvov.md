Prince Georgy Lvov was born in Dresden into Russian royalty. On March 15, 1917 he became the first prime minister of the Russian Republic. 

He was imprisoned for three months in Yekaterinburg. His bolshevist prison guard, a former piano maker from Petrograd, developed a liking to the fallen prince and let him enact agrarian reforms in the prison. Without any evidence to tie him to a counter-revolutionary conspiracy the Prince was freed and fled to Omsk, at that point, in control of the [[Provisional Siberian Government]].

After Russia was "destroyed" he lost faith in its power to rebuild itself:
"In diesem Sinne ist er [der Bolschewismus] eine russische Krankheit und kann deshalb nur durch ausländische Interventionen kuriert werden." - Oct. 12, 1918 to American businessman Charles Crane
